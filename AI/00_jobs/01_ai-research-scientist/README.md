# 01 · AI / ML Research Scientist

- **Type:** Full-time · frontier labs & big-tech research orgs
- **In one line:** Invent the algorithms and training recipes behind frontier models — architectures, pre/post-training, reasoning, alignment.
- **Where (examples):** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta (FAIR/GenAI), xAI, Mistral, Cohere, Microsoft Research, Ai2.

## 🎯 What the work is
- Push the research frontier: new architectures, objectives, scaling, reasoning, alignment methods.
- Design and run large experiments; read results; publish or ship into the next model.
- Turn a fuzzy research question into a measurable hypothesis and a training run.

## 🧰 Core skills
- Deep ML theory + math (optimization, probability, deep learning); strong PyTorch.
- Ability to design experiments and reason about scaling/ablations.
- Usually a PhD or equivalent research track record (papers, novel results).

## 📈 Market note
A tiny, extremely competitive tier — a few thousand people globally, concentrated at frontier labs. Reasoning, agents, and alignment are the hottest 2025–26 research directions.
