# 06 · GenAI Engineer (Generative AI Engineer)

- **Type:** Full-time
- **In one line:** Build generative-AI features on top of foundation models — RAG, chatbots, summarization, content generation, multimodal — for products and enterprises.
- **Where (examples):** Enterprises adopting GenAI, product companies, IT-services & consultancies (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Deloitte), and most AI startups. In India this is one of the most-advertised AI titles.

## 🎯 What the work is
- Integrate foundation models via APIs; build RAG, chat, summarization, extraction, content-gen.
- Add retrieval (vector/graph), guardrails, and evals; sometimes light fine-tuning.
- Ship and operate GenAI features: quality, latency, cost, and safety in production.

## 🧰 Core skills
- Backend/full-stack + applied LLM patterns (prompting, RAG, structured output).
- Vector DBs, embeddings, evals; pragmatic model/pattern selection.

## 📈 Market note
The **broadest, most common AI title** — and it's **converging into "Applied AI Engineer" / "AI Systems Engineer"** ([#05](../05_applied-ai-llm-product-engineer/README.md)) as prompting gets bundled into wider requirements. Listed separately because you'll see "GenAI Engineer" on a huge share of listings, especially in India. Treat #05 and #06 as near-synonyms; #04 (Agentic) is the more autonomous variant.
