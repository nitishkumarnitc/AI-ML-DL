# 10 · Forward-Deployed / AI Solutions Engineer (FDE)

- **Type:** Full-time
- **In one line:** Embed with customers to design, build, and ship AI solutions on top of the product — half engineer, half consultant.
- **Where (examples):** Palantir (originated the FDE role), OpenAI, Anthropic, Sierra, Harvey, Glean, Scale, Ramp, and most enterprise-focused AI startups.

## 🎯 What the work is
- Sit with a customer, understand their workflow, and build a working AI solution fast.
- Integrate APIs/agents/RAG into messy real environments; own it end to end.
- Feed product/eng what's missing; high autonomy, high customer contact.

## 🧰 Core skills
- Strong full-stack + applied LLM; fast in unfamiliar codebases; excellent communication.
- Comfort with ambiguity and direct customer work.

## 📈 Market note
One of the **fastest-growing** roles at AI-native startups (2024→). Great fit for senior generalist engineers who like shipping and talking to users.
