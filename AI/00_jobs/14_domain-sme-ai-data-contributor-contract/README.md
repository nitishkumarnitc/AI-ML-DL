# 14 · Domain SME / AI Data Contributor — Contract

- **Type:** Remote contractor
- **In one line:** Contribute your domain expertise (finance, healthcare, law, STEM, coding) as training data, evals, and feedback for frontier models.
- **Where (examples):** Outlier (Scale), Mercor, Micro1, Handshake AI, Surge AI, Invisible, DataAnnotation, Prolific, Turing.

## 🎯 What the work is
- Write, review, or grade model outputs in your area of expertise; craft hard questions/tasks.
- Produce reference answers and rubrics; flag where the model is subtly wrong.
- Feed the human-preference / verifiable-reward data that post-training relies on.

## 🧰 Core skills
- Genuine domain depth; clear writing; reliability and consistency.
- For coding/STEM SMEs: solid engineering + ability to specify correctness precisely.

## 📈 Market note
**The lowest barrier to entry** and the on-ramp many people use to break into the AI-data world. Coding SMEs often graduate into [RL Env / Task Author](../12_rl-environment-task-author-contract/README.md) work.

## 📚 Path in this repo
- Bring your own domain; for the *coding/agent* domain, use [`10_rl-environments-and-infra`](../../10_rl-environments-and-infra/README.md) and [`16_evals`](../../16_evals/README.md) to learn how your contributions become gradable signal.

## 🎒 How to stand out
- Precision and consistency; for technical domains, show you can turn "correct" into a checkable spec.
