# 10 — Questions to Ask Them & Red Flags

> A Principal candidate who doesn't interrogate the role is a red flag *to them*. Great questions also surface whether this job is real or a founder's wish-list. Ask 2–4 per round, tuned to the interviewer.

---

## 🎤 Questions by interviewer

### To the CTO / hiring manager (vision + reality)
- "What does the AI platform look like today vs. the vision? What's actually in production?"
- "Where is the current AI effort — greenfield, or evolving existing systems? What's the biggest bottleneck right now?"
- "This role partners with you directly — how do you see the split between hands-on IC work and technical leadership? What % coding do you expect in year one?"
- "What does 'setting the AI engineering bar for the org' mean concretely — what's missing today?"
- "How is AI success measured here — business metrics, not just model metrics?"
- "The core is Java/Spring; AI is Python. How do you see that boundary evolving, and how much friction is there today?"
- "What's the biggest AI-related bet that could fail, and what would failure look like?"

### To engineers / peers (day-to-day truth)
- "Walk me through how an AI feature goes from idea to production today. What's painful?"
- "What's the current state of eval, observability, and guardrails? Ad hoc or systematized?"
- "How much of the 'agentic AI' is live vs. roadmap?"
- "What's the on-call / incident story for AI services?"
- "Where do you wish you had more senior technical leadership?"

### On domain / regulatory (shows fintech seriousness)
- "How do compliance and auditability constraints shape AI system design here?"
- "What regulatory bodies/requirements most affect what we can do with LLMs on financial data?"
- "How do you handle explainability requirements for AI-influenced lending/credit decisions?"

### On team / org / growth
- "How is the AI/ML org structured — platform vs. embedded? Is there an applied-science/research function I'd partner with?"
- "What's the team size and hiring plan? Would I be building a team or joining one?"
- "How are technical decisions and architecture reviews made today?"
- "What does growth look like from Principal here — is there a track beyond?"

### On scope / expectations (protect yourself)
- "What are the top 2–3 outcomes you'd want from me in the first 6 months?"
- "What's the on-call, ownership, and decision-authority scope of this role?"

---

## 🚩 Red / yellow flags to watch for

- **"Foundational AI leadership role" + tiny/no existing AI team** → could be great 0→1, or could be a solo hero-project with no support. Probe for backing, budget, and realistic timelines.
- **Vision >> reality** — lots of "agentic AI transforming debt markets," little in production. Ask what's *live*. Fine if honestly greenfield; risky if they think it's further along than it is.
- **Java-core org treating AI as a bolt-on** — will you have real authority and resources, or be a token AI hire fighting the mothership? Gauge executive commitment.
- **Unrealistic hands-on + leadership + hiring + strategy** in one role → burnout risk. Clarify priorities and support.
- **Comp/leveling mismatch** — is "Principal" real scope + comp, or an inflated title? → next section.
- **No eval/guardrail maturity + high-stakes financial decisions in prod** → either a huge opportunity to build it right, or a liability if they're already shipping recklessly. Find out which.
- **High attrition / recent reorgs / funding pressure** — fintech unicorns face down-round and profitability pressure. Ask about runway/stability tactfully.
- **Vague answers about decision authority** for a "Principal" → you may not actually own architecture.

---

## 💰 Comp & leveling notes (India, Principal/Staff AI)

- **Establish level = scope + comp early.** "Principal" varies wildly. Confirm it maps to Staff/Principal band (org-wide technical influence), not a senior with a fancy title.
- **India Principal/Staff at a funded fintech unicorn:** expect a strong base + meaningful **ESOPs/RSUs** — the equity is a real (and risk-bearing) part; understand strike price, vesting, liquidity story, latest valuation (down-rounds matter).
- **Ask for the full picture:** base, variable/bonus, equity (%, vesting, cliff, current 409A/valuation basis), joining bonus.
- **Leverage:** you were *headhunted* — that's negotiating strength. You're not desperate; you're evaluating fit. Let them make the case.
- **Competing signal:** if you have or can generate other conversations, it strengthens your position — but never bluff.
- **AI-specialist premium:** production LLM/agent talent is scarce; price accordingly.
