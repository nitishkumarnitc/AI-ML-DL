# Acme Support FAQ

Acme is a fictional consumer electronics retailer. This FAQ covers the most
common account and order questions. (All content here is generic sample data.)

## Support hours and contact

Acme support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time,
excluding public holidays. You can reach us by email at support@acme.example or
through live chat on the website during support hours. Typical email response
time is one business day.

## Creating and managing an account

You can shop as a guest, but creating an Acme account lets you track orders,
save addresses, and start returns online. To create an account, choose "Sign
up" and provide your email address and a password.

## Resetting your password

If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in
page. We email a secure link that lets you reset your password. Reset links
expire after 60 minutes for security. If you do not receive the email, check
your spam folder or contact support.

## Tracking an order

After an order ships, we email a tracking number and a link to the carrier's
tracking page. You can also view real-time order status under "My Orders" in
your account. Orders are usually processed within one business day of purchase.

## Updating or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order from "My Orders" while its status is still
"Processing". Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but it
can be returned under the standard returns policy.
