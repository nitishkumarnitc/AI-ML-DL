# Acme Shipping Information

## Domestic shipping options and times

Acme offers two domestic shipping speeds, calculated from the ship date (orders
usually ship within one business day):

- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
- Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

## Shipping cost and free shipping

Standard shipping is a flat rate for orders under the free-shipping threshold.
Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping. Express shipping is
available for an additional fee regardless of order value.

## International shipping

Acme ships internationally to most countries. International delivery typically
takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination and customs processing.
Import duties and taxes may apply on delivery and are the customer's
responsibility.

## Tracking

Every shipment includes a tracking number, emailed when the order ships and
also available under "My Orders". Please allow up to 24 hours after shipment
for the first tracking scan to appear.
