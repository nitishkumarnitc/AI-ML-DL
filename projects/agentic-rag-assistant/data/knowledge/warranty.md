# Acme Warranty

## Coverage

Acme products include a 12-month limited warranty, valid for one year from the
date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship
under normal use.

## What is covered

If a product fails due to a manufacturing defect within the warranty period,
Acme will repair or replace it at no charge, including return shipping for the
defective item.

## What is not covered

The warranty excludes accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modification,
normal wear and tear, and consumable parts such as batteries after their rated
life. Damage from liquids or drops is not covered unless a separate protection
plan was purchased.

## How to make a warranty claim

To file a claim, contact support with your order number and a description of
the issue. Support may request photos or a short troubleshooting session.
Approved claims receive a prepaid shipping label, and a repaired or replacement
unit is typically sent within 7-10 business days of our receiving the product.
